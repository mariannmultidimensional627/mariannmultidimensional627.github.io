👋 What Is luka?

luka is a small application that listens to the sound coming out of your computer and turns that sound into words you can read on screen. If a video, a meeting, a podcast, or a phone call is playing on your machine, luka writes it down for you as it happens.

Think of it as a friendly note-taker that never gets tired. It can show live captions while a clip plays, save a full written transcript you can read later, tell you which person said which sentence, and even translate what it hears into another language.

You do not need to know anything about code, terminals, or settings files. You download it, you open it, you press start. That is the whole idea.

🖥️ Before You Begin

A few small things make the first launch much smoother. None of them take more than a minute.

  • Give it a little space. The application itself is small, but saved transcripts take up room over time. A couple of gigabytes of free disk space is plenty.
  • Have an internet connection ready. The speech recognition and translation work is handled online, so luka needs to be able to reach the internet while it is listening.
  • Expect a permission request. The first time luka starts, your computer will ask whether it may access audio. Choose to allow it, or luka will have nothing to listen to.
  • Turn the volume up a little. luka hears what your computer plays, so if the playback is almost silent, the words will be hard to catch.

🎯 How to Use luka

Once the application is running, the workflow is short and the same every time:

  1. Open luka the same way you open any other program on your computer.
  2. Press the start button. You will see the app move into a listening state.
  3. Play something. Start the video, meeting, or podcast you want written down.
  4. Watch the words appear. Captions show up in the luka window as the audio plays.
  5. Press stop when you are done. Your transcript is ready to read, copy, or save.

💡 Tips for the Best Results

  • Clean audio gives clean text. Background hiss, music, and echo are the three things that confuse any speech recognition tool.
  • One voice at a time is easiest. When several people talk over each other, expect a few messy sentences.
  • Specialist words can come out wrong. Names, brands, and technical terms are guesswork for any machine, so read those parts with a little patience.
  • If the captions lag, close heavy programs in the background. A busy computer gives less attention to live captions.
  • Save your transcripts somewhere you will find them again. A folder called "Transcripts" is a good habit.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

The download will not start

Refresh the page and try again, and make sure a download blocker or pop-up blocker is not quietly stopping it. If you are on a work or school network, try a different connection.

The app opens but hears nothing

This is almost always a permission problem. Open your system settings, find the privacy or security section, and make sure luka is allowed to access audio. Then close the app completely and open it again.

The computer warns about an unknown app

Computers warn about anything downloaded from the internet. Open your system settings, look for the security section, and confirm that you want to open the application. This is normal for small independent tools.

The captions stop halfway

Check that your internet connection is still alive. luka relies on the network for the hard part of the work, and a brief drop will pause the words until the connection comes back.
